STAY PLAYFUL ↗

A quick warm-up

One ball. Endless “again.”

Press Start playing, then click or tap the moving ball. Each touch gives it a new direction and adds one to your score. Click the turf and your accuracy drops.

  1. Free play: move at your own pace, for as long as you like.
  2. 30s sprint: get as many touches as you can before the clock runs out. Your best completed sprint is saved in this browser when storage is available.
  3. Your controls: adjust speed at any time. Space plays or pauses when you’re not using a control; R resets. You can also Tab to the ball and press Enter to kick.

Switching mode starts a fresh session. Leaving the tab pauses your game automatically. No account, no downloads, no pressure.
